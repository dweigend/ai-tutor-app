## 2. Detailed Media & Content Provenance Notes

### 2.1 3Blue1Brown & External Educational Channels

Selected explanations and exercises in the _„KI selbst bauen“_ demonstration course address foundational deep-learning concepts (gradient descent, backpropagation along computational graphs, scaled dot-product attention) inspired by Grant Sanderson's [3Blue1Brown Deep Learning series](https://www.3blue1brown.com/about/). To maintain strict copyright and licensing hygiene:

1. Every German explanation, interactive Three.js scene (`valley-scene.ts`, `relief-scene.ts`), formula reading, and exercise in `src/` was written and programmed independently from scratch.
2. Zero video frames, audio segments, subtitle/transcript files, or Manim code from 3Blue1Brown or any other external creator are included in this repository.
3. In `/magazin`, the 10 external video and podcast entries store only public bibliographic metadata (`videoId`, original title, channel name/URL, duration) plus original German editorial context notes and reflection prompts (`topicNote`, `thinkPrompt`).

### 2.2 Bundled Webfonts & Glyph Verification

The font files in `static/fonts/` are self-hosted WOFF2 subsets built reproducibly via `scripts/fonts/build-fonts.sh` (`fonttools` + `brotli`) from upstream SIL Open Font License 1.1 sources. No reserved font names are modified in derivative redistribution, and the original `OFL-Archivo.txt` and `OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt` license texts are shipped alongside the WOFF2 files in `static/fonts/`. UI icons use the project's own inline SVG vector set (`src/lib/modules/core/icons.ts`); no third-party icon font is shipped.

### 2.3 GSAP Licensing Boundary

Within `ai-tutor-app`, `gsap` (`^3.15.1`) is confined to home-screen tile choreography (`src/lib/components/TileGrid.svelte`); `scripts/check-modules.mjs` forbids importing `gsap` inside `src/lib/modules/`. Downstream users who fork `ai-tutor-app` for free educational, academic, or open-access deployments fall under GreenSock's Standard "No Charge" License (`https://gsap.com/standard-license`). Organizations embedding the home-grid animation into a paid SaaS product charged to end users must obtain the appropriate commercial GreenSock license or swap `TileGrid.svelte` to the MIT-licensed `src/lib/modules/core/motion.ts` spring integrator.
